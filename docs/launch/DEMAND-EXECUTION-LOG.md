1. Show HN / Product Hunt / Reddit (one launch day) — largest spike
2. Awesome-list merges — steady drip + SEO
3. Seed DMs (15–25 people who try it) — early social proof before Day 0
4. Repeat demo posts with the Tanoa GIF
